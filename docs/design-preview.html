Learning to own
what I build

Parth Sharma · A sample of the blog layout

There’s a particular satisfaction in opening a website and knowing that you understand the pieces underneath it. Not every line of code, necessarily, but enough to follow a request from the browser to the server and back.

That’s the part I’m interested in now. Slowing down, asking better questions, and making the things I build a little less mysterious.

The goal is to understand enough to make the next decision myself.

These notes are a place to keep that process: what worked, what surprised me, and what I’d do differently next time.
